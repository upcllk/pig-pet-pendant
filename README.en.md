# Pig Pet Pendant

A small desktop pig pendant that follows your mouse. The macOS version is available; a Windows version is planned but not yet available.

![Preview of six modes](docs/media/modes.gif)

*The preview shows all six modes in sequence. The images were rendered by the app and combined automatically; they are not a screen recording.*

## Modes

- **Rope Pendant**: The default mode. The pig reacts to movement and falling, with randomly spaced blinks, ear movements, and body gestures at rest. After the rope settles, a body gesture can nudge the pig into a short swing; its amplitude is adjustable from 0–200% in Settings. Actions return smoothly to rest instead of looping the entire GIF.
- **Pig Balloons**: Shows 1–8 pig-shaped balloons floating upward; the default is 2. Each has independently timed blinks, gentle ear movements and nose gestures, with movement reactions.
- **Pig in a Net**: A pig in a net with a looping animation and local runtime cleanup of white/gray mesh fringes.
- **Pig on Bamboo**: The pig hugs a bamboo stalk that bends with mouse movement, with random blinks, ear movements and nose gestures that preserve its grip.
- **Hanging Pig**: Hangs from a rope with randomly timed ear twitches and nose gestures. Its original halo lags slightly during movement, rebounds after stopping, and occasionally tilts at rest. Cross eyes do not blink; the band, attachment, and original halo artwork are preserved.
- **Ghost Pig**: Follows the mouse smoothly without a rope.

## Music Reactive Experiment

At launch, the app detects drum onsets from changes in low- and mid-frequency energy in system playback audio. The menu-bar icon grows on detected hits and falls back between them; the desktop pendant is unaffected. This is a real-time onset estimate, not full-song beat-grid or music recognition. On macOS 14.2 or later, first launch requests **System Audio Recording** permission solely to analyze playback locally in real time for drum onsets that drive the menu-bar icon; audio is not recorded or saved, and **Screen Recording** permission is not requested. The current permission or capture status is available in the status-item menu.

## Usage

Click the pig icon in the menu bar to switch modes or open **Settings**. Settings are saved automatically. Closing the Settings window does not quit the app; select **Quit Pig Pet Pendant** to exit.

## Build

Requires macOS 12 or later and Xcode Command Line Tools. From the repository root, run:

```bash
./build.sh
open dist/PigPendant.app
```

The app bundle and a ZIP archive for the current architecture are created in `dist/`. The app uses an ad hoc signature and is not notarized by Apple. When opened on another Mac for the first time, macOS may show a security warning.

To run the build, physics, and GUI smoke checks:

```bash
./build.sh --test
```

GUI checks require a logged-in desktop session. The test pendant appears briefly and exits automatically.

GUI checks cover normal-size gestures, protected cross eyes/band/attachment, disjoint halo/body pixels, and color/alpha preservation. Halo amplitude has been tuned following user feedback. This iteration raises movement angle/offset targets by a further 30% and random plane travel by about 40%, without changing gaps, timing, or damping. Movement angle targets are now capped at about 12.5 degrees, with an idle target peak of about 9.5 degrees; rebound may slightly overshoot. A fixed-input render check at 48 pt / 2x requires more than 3.75 pt of halo-center displacement without clipping or alpha loss. Displacement still depends on movement speed.

Idle halo gestures also wander along two oblique axes in the pig's local head plane, with randomized starting directions and a smooth curved return. This is two-dimensional visual motion, without scaling or a claim of 3D simulation. Quiet gaps and movement lag/rebound remain. `dist/hanging-halo-plane.png` shows varied idle poses.

Runtime body cleanup now selects and deletes white pixels instead of repainting them with skin colors. A four-connected selection grows from original transparency through eligible white pixels, limited to three source pixels from original transparency to protect interior facial features and the band. In unpremultiplied RGB, minimum channel values of at least 0.78 with chroma at most 0.22 are deleted; the 0.68–0.78 range smoothly reduces coverage. Other colors remain unchanged. All 62 net frames use the same contiguous white selection/deletion, clearing fringes beside transparent mesh holes and outer contours instead of painting them blue. Interior selection is protected; holes are not filled, alpha never increases, and nonwhite mesh pixels are unchanged. The source APNG, canvases, order, and 40 ms timing remain intact. GUI checks verify selected boundary whites become transparent in every frame and the app loads the processed frames, exporting `dist/net-edge-comparison.png`. Unprotected global white deletion was also tried, but visibly cut holes around features, the band, and mesh/face intersections; the user chose interior protection. Interior highlights remain, so this is not a claim of removing every white pixel globally. Hanging Pig's body has not been replaced.

## Known Limitations

- A Windows executable is not currently available.
- Balloons do not collide with each other and are not constrained by screen boundaries.
- macOS has no supported API for reading another app's global cursor visibility, so the pendant may remain visible over fullscreen video after the cursor is hidden.
- The app does not request Accessibility or Screen Recording permissions and does not send network requests.
